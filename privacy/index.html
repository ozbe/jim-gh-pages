Privacy policy

Short version: your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

What Jim collects

Jim doesn't collect your data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking, and it doesn't send your information to me or anyone else. I don't run a server for Jim.

Where your data is kept

Your plan, your settings, the exercises you add, your workouts, and your history are stored on your iPhone, Apple Watch, and Mac, and shared between them and the Jim widgets.

To keep your devices in step, Jim syncs that data through your own private iCloud account, encrypted. Only you can see it there; I can't. If you're not signed in to iCloud, Jim still works, and your iPhone and Apple Watch still sync directly with each other.

Apple Health

  • Jim reads your cardio workouts from Apple Health so they count toward your week.
  • On Apple Watch, Jim reads your heart rate during strength workouts.
  • Jim saves the workouts you do in it to Apple Health, from both iPhone and Apple Watch. Workouts done on Apple Watch count toward your Activity rings.

Health data is only used inside Jim to do these things. It is never used for advertising, never shared with data brokers, and never leaves your devices: heart rate and the cardio Jim reads from Health aren't synced to iCloud. You can change or remove Jim's access any time in Settings, then Health, then Data Access & Devices.

Sharing a workout

You can share a workout as a link. The link holds that workout's name and its exercises, sets, reps, and rest times, including the names of any exercises you added. It never includes your weights, your history, or anything from Health. It only goes to the people you send it to.

When someone opens the link, the workout is read in their browser. It sits after the # in the link, which browsers never send to a website, so this site never sees it.

Links to other sites

Jim can open a YouTube search for an exercise in your browser. That is a normal web link, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies once you're there.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, I'll update this page. Questions? Email jim@ozbe.dev.
