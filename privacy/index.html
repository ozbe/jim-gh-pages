Privacy policy

Short version: your data stays on your devices.

What Jim collects

Jim doesn't collect your data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking, and it doesn't send your information to me or anyone else. I don't run a server for Jim.

What stays on your devices

Your plan, your workouts, and your history are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and shared between them and the Jim widgets.

Apple Health

  • Jim reads your cardio workouts from Apple Health so they count toward your week.
  • On Apple Watch, Jim reads your heart rate during strength workouts.
  • Jim saves your strength workouts to Apple Health so they count toward your Activity rings.

Health data is only used inside Jim to do these things. It is never used for advertising, never shared with data brokers, and never leaves your devices. You can change or remove Jim's access any time in Settings, then Health, then Data Access & Devices.

Links to other sites

Jim can open a YouTube search for an exercise in your browser. That is a normal web link, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies once you're there.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, I'll update this page. Questions? Email jim@ozbe.dev.
